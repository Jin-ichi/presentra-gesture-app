Gesture Tutorial

✊ Fist Instantly pauses tracking while talking or adjusting.
✋ Steady Palm (1s) Hold still for 1 second to unpause and resume control.
⬅️ ➡️ Swipe Left / Right Move hand horizontally to go to previous or next slide.
✌️ Victory (2 Fingers) Toggles Pointer Mode. Move with index finger; pinch thumb + index to click.
🤏 Pinch / Spread Spread thumb & index to Zoom In; pinch together to Zoom Out.
☝️ Point Up / 🤙 Pinky Up Point up for Volume Up; raise pinky alone for Volume Down.
Tips: Keep your hand inside the comfortable camera range and ensure even front lighting so landmarks stay steady.
